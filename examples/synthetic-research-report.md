# Choosing a venue for a team workshop

**Fictional illustration: the venues, documents, search and findings are invented. No real availability or price was checked.**

**Venue B meets the documented space and budget requirements, but availability on the workshop date is unconfirmed. A booking decision is still open.**

The project is a workshop for 20 people with a EUR 1,000 venue budget. The agenda is agreed; this task compared venue options.

| Venue | Finding in the fictional documents |
| --- | --- |
| A | Seats 16; below the required capacity. |
| **B** | Seats 24; quoted room total EUR 900 including tax; date unconfirmed. |
| C | Seats 30; quoted room total EUR 1,200 including tax; above budget. |

I compared the three supplied venue sheets and their quotes against capacity and the room budget. Refreshments were excluded from all quotes, so this comparison covers room hire only. No venues outside that shortlist were searched and no bookings or enquiries were made.

Venue B is the candidate to check for availability. The supplied material supports its capacity and room cost, but cannot establish whether it can host the workshop on the required date.

In a real report, each venue finding would link to its actual sheet or dated quote. This example has no real sources or deliverables.
